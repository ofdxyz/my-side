Welcome to Our Nebula

Our Nebula is just my little corner for sharing the space photos I take. Some are shot through a telescope, some with just my phone. The Moon, planets, the Milky Way, a faint galaxy if the sky is kind that night.

I started this because I like showing people what’s actually up there. Every picture here is something I stood outside in the dark and waited for. I hope you enjoy looking at them as much as I enjoyed taking them.
